Security and control

Inside your environment. A person approves every output.

The questions a CTO asks on the second call, answered on the first. This page is written for the person who keeps the systems running.

Where it runs

Your tenant, your DMZ or your own server.

Your cloud tenant.

A dedicated, restricted subscription inside your own cloud account, set up with your integrator. Storage and document processing in region. Model calls with no data retention.

Your DMZ.

A gateway in your DMZ and a read-only API layer over your ERP. Only the question text, and audio for transcription, leaves.

On-prem.

A dedicated server inside your network, reached over your VPN, with your warehouse and delivery systems as inputs.

What it can touch

Read-only by default.

Access.

Read-only on your systems unless you decide otherwise, per system, per process. No write access to your core system in a pilot.

What leaves.

Written down before we connect anything: which fields, to which service, for what. No named individuals in model calls where the process allows placeholders.

Who approves.

A named person per process. The agent proposes, the person approves. Nothing is sent, assigned or decided on its own.

What is logged.

Every run, every action, every decision and every rule change, with a date and a name. A daily plan-versus-actual comparison where the process has one.

Before code

Three documents, signed before the first line of code.

01

Red lines.

What the agent never does. Written by you, kept by us.

02

Stop rule.

When we pause, in numbers. For example: three factual errors in twenty cases.

03

Success bar.

The number that has to move and by how much, agreed in advance.

Privacy Amendment 13

MateOS is built to Israel's Privacy Protection Law as amended by Amendment 13: data minimization, purpose limitation, access logging, a named responsible person on our side, and processing inside your environment. We document the data flows for each process before it goes live and hand you the documentation.

Security questionnaires

We answer your security questionnaire before the engagement agreement is signed, and we work with your integrator on the environment. Write to hello@mateos.ai with the questionnaire and the name of the person who owns it on your side.
