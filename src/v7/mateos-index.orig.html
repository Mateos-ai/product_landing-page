What it does

An example process, end to end.

New lead, qualify, quote, follow up, closed deal. Today each step is a person reading something in one system and keying it into another, or waiting for an answer from the office. MateOS carries the step, writes the record, chases the follow-up and stops at the exception.

  • Access. Read-only on your systems unless you decide otherwise. One connection per system, not a new database.
  • Monitoring. Which agent ran, on what, with what result. Every action logged.
  • Rules. Yours, in plain language. Max 10% discount. No PO, no payment. Oncology stays manual.
  • Exception desk. When the rule does not cover the case, a person decides. The decision becomes the next rule.
  • Learning loop. Your processes get better with every run, because every exception solved once stays solved.

Questions owners ask

Before the first call.

Do my people need to learn a new tool?

No. The work stays in the ERP, the mailbox and WhatsApp. MateOS runs underneath.

What happens when the system is not sure?

It stops and asks. The exception goes to the person you name, with the context. Their decision becomes a rule if you want it to.

Where does our data run?

Inside your own environment: your cloud tenant, your DMZ or your own server. We write down what leaves and why before we connect anything.

What if it makes a mistake?

Every output is approved by a person before it acts. A stop rule is agreed before we start: for example, three factual errors in twenty cases and the pilot pauses.

What does it cost?

The X-ray is from $990. Pilot and Processes are scoped per process, on a number you already watch. Part of our fee rides on that number.

What if we prefer a fixed fee?

Some owners do. The combination of a base fee and a success share is the offer, not a rule.

Is there a person we can call?

Always. The agents run behind the scenes; the people stay in the room, before the first process and after the tenth.
