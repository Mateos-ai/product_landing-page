Built backwards, on purpose.

Most projects start with tools and work up to the number. Mate starts with the number and works down.

Six screens. One goal.

Product preview. Screens and numbers are sample data.

Type the goal. Mate turns it into a metric, baseline and deadline.

Product preview: setting a goal. The owner types 'Get 90% of quotes out within 4 hours by January 15.' Mate reads it as a metric with a 6% baseline from the last 90 days, a 90% target by January 15 and Dana K. as owner, then asks who approves large quotes and whether discounts above 10% need a manager.

Mate drafts every step. You review it before anything goes live.

Product preview: the flow Mate built from the goal, seven steps: a quote request arrives in the mailbox, Mate matches the customer and checks stock in the ERP read-only, prices within rule R-01, drafts the quote, Dana K. approves, and it is sent and logged. Discounts over 10% go to the exception desk. Status: ready to review.

One number, checked daily. Drift comes with a proposed fix.

Product preview: monitoring the goal. Today 51% against a plan of 48%. Mate’s forecast is 78% by January 15, so it flags the goal at risk: large orders wait for a stock check. Dana K. approves Mate’s fix to check stock in parallel; the forecast rises to 91% and the goal is back on track.

When no rule covers the case, the person you name decides.

Product preview: the exception desk. Customer #4471 asks for 14% on 600 units while rule R-01 allows 10%. Mate suggests 12% for orders over 500 units because the customer pays on time. Dana K. approves and saves it as rule R-14.

Plain-language rules you can read, change and switch off.

Product preview: the rules list in plain language, including the new rule R-14 from today’s exception and R-11 from the monitor fix. The owner changes R-03 so quotes above $40,000 go to the owner. Red lines: never writes to the ERP, never replies to a customer without approval, never changes a rule on its own.

Every action, decision and rule change, with a date and a name.

Product preview: the audit trail for today, every action by Mate and by people, quotes drafted, approved and sent, rules added and changed, each with a time and a name. A filter shows only the rule changes.
